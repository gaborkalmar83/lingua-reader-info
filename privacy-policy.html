Privacy Policy - Bilingua Reader

Last updated: 2026-11-26. Covers Bilingua Reader for Android (package com.lingua.reader), version 5.12.

Bilingua Reader is published by Kalmarium. Contact: kalmariumservices@gmail.com

The short version

  • There is no Bilingua Reader account and no server run by the developer. The developer does not receive your books, the text you read, your words, notes, reading statistics, API keys or sync data.
  • By default, translation runs on your device and your reading text is not sent anywhere.
  • Text leaves your device only when you use a feature that needs an online service. Each one is listed below, with what it receives. In those cases the text goes straight from your device to that service.
  • No advertising. No analytics or tracking software of our own. Google libraries inside the app (ML Kit, Play services and Play Billing) send Google their own technical diagnostics; see "Advertising, analytics and diagnostics".
  • The app is a one-off purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment.

Data stored on your device

All of this stays in the app's private storage unless you turn on a sync or export it yourself:

  • your books (EPUB, PDF, imported web pages and shared text) and their covers;
  • reading positions, bookmarks, notes, saved words and sentences, practice history;
  • the translation cache (translations already made, so they are not requested twice);
  • reading statistics and per-provider usage figures (calls, characters or tokens, response times, last error);
  • settings, including API keys, tokens and server passwords you enter. These secrets are encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore and are not included in settings backups or Drive sync;
  • downloaded language packs and AI models;
  • audio clips generated by your phone's text-to-speech engine for flashcards;
  • the trial start date and a "purchased" flag (see "Purchase and trial");
  • an optional debug log. It is off by default and stays on the device unless you choose to share it. It can contain technical details; the Google account address is stored in a masked form only.

If the app crashes, the error is shown on screen for you to copy. It is not sent anywhere.

Android backup. If Android backup is on for your device, Android may include the app's database and settings (not books, covers, models, the translation cache or the debug log) in your own Google backup. That backup is run by Android and Google, not by the developer.

Translation

On your device (default). Google ML Kit language packs, the OPUS-MT models, and the optional on-device AI models translate without sending your text anywhere. Downloading a pack or model contacts Google (ML Kit) or Hugging Face (OPUS-MT, Qwen, Gemma and the other on-device models) to fetch the file. No reading text is part of that request.

Online providers (only if you select one). The sentences on the pages you read, plus the language pair, are sent directly from your device to the provider you chose. If you turn on background translation or whole-book translation for a book, the text of that whole book is sent.

Dictation and voice chat. If you tap the microphone (notes, sentence check, page chat), the app asks for microphone permission and uses Android's speech recognition. It prefers on-device recognition. If that is not available for the language, your phone's default speech service (on most phones, Google's) processes the audio online. The app does not record, store or send audio itself.

Read-aloud. Text is spoken by the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. If you pick a voice that needs a network connection, that engine's provider receives the text.

Connections to the hosted services named in this policy use HTTPS. Plain HTTP is possible only for servers you add yourself (calibre, OPDS, KOReader, a custom translation server), for example on your home network.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you sign in with Google, the app asks for:

  • drive.appdata - a private app folder in your own Google Drive that only Bilingua Reader can access and that does not show in your Drive file list;
  • your Google account email address, used only on the device to show which account is connected.

Stored in that folder, according to the categories you leave on in Settings: library details and reading positions, the book files themselves and their covers, saved words, notes, cached translations, reading statistics, and settings (without API keys or passwords).

Notes in Google Docs (optional, separate switch). If you turn this on, the app additionally asks for documents and drive.file and writes your notes into Google Docs documents it creates in your Drive. drive.file limits the app's Drive access to files it created.

None of this is sent to the developer.

Purchase and trial

The app has a 14-day free trial and a one-off in-app purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app never sees your card or payment details. It receives the purchase record from Google Play on your device and checks it there; the app does not send it to the developer. Like every Play developer, Kalmarium can see order information (such as order number, country and price) in the Google Play Console.

To keep the trial from restarting after a reinstall, the trial start date is stored on the device and in Google Play services "Block Store", which can back it up with your Google account. It contains dates only, no identifier. To tie the purchased flag to your device, the app computes a hash from the device's app-specific Android ID and the app's signature. The hash stays on the device.

Advertising, analytics and diagnostics

Bilingua Reader contains no advertising and no advertising ID use. We run no analytics or tracking software of our own and no crash-reporting service, and the developer receives no usage data. The Google libraries named below send Google their own diagnostics (technical data and a per-installation identifier, for app functionality and analytics), as declared in the Play Data safety section.

Some third-party libraries inside the app can send their own technical diagnostics to their makers:

  • Google ML Kit, Google Play Billing and Google Play services. According to Google's documentation, these can send Google technical data such as device and app information, a per-installation identifier, performance figures and error codes. They do not send the text you read.

AI transparency (EU AI Act)

Bilingua Reader is a translation and language-learning aid. Under the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) this is a minimal or limited-risk use. It is not used for biometric identification or categorisation, emotion recognition, employment, credit, law enforcement, or any automated decision with a legal or similarly significant effect on a person, and it does no profiling of its users.

Where AI is used:

  • Not AI-generated: dictionary lookups (Wiktionary, dictionaryapi.dev, DuckDuckGo) and LanguageTool's rule-based grammar check. Translation by ML Kit, OPUS-MT, DeepL, MyMemory or a custom server is machine translation of existing text, not open-ended generation.
  • Generated by a large language model, on your device (Qwen, Gemma or another model you download) or by a hosted provider you connected with your own key (OpenRouter, Azure AI Foundry, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini):
    • translations, when one of those models is your translation provider, or when OPUS-MT's "quality" mode polishes sentences with the on-device model;
    • AI word explanations;
    • AI grammar breakdowns;
    • the page chat;
    • "Check my sentence".

How it is labelled in the app:

  • In the reader, translations written by a language model show "AI-generated translation" in the top bar and on the page.
  • AI word explanations, AI grammar breakdowns, the page chat and "Check my sentence" results show "AI-generated".
  • A LanguageTool grammar result is marked as coming from the web checker instead.
  • The "Translate in Bilingua Reader" pop-up and the fallback word translation show the label too. It is not repeated on translations you save or export (saved words, notes, Anki cards).
  • Next to AI answers in the page chat, word explanations and grammar breakdowns there is a "Report this answer" button. It opens a drafted email in your own mail app; you see what it contains and send it yourself.

AI output can be wrong. Machine translation is best effort and not a substitute for a human translation.

International transfers

The services above are operated by companies in various countries, including the United States. When you choose to use one, your device sends the data directly to it, and that provider's terms and privacy policy apply, including for any transfer outside your country. The developer does not transfer your data anywhere, because the developer does not receive it.

Deleting your data

  • On the device: delete individual books, words and notes in the app, or uninstall the app (or clear its storage in Android settings) to remove everything.
  • Google Drive: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings - Manage apps - Bilingua Reader - "Delete hidden app data". Delete the notes documents like any other Drive file. Remove the app's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Other services (translation and AI providers, Hardcover, KOReader sync, your own servers): delete data and API keys through that service.
  • Trial and purchase records are held by Google Play under your Google account.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection. The developer holds no personal data about you from the app, so in practice you exercise these rights yourself: your data is on your device and in your own accounts, and you can export it (Settings - Backup, and the Notes and Saved screens) or delete it as described above. For data held by a third-party service, contact that service. If you email the developer, that email is used only to answer you. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Bilingua Reader is not directed at children and the developer does not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age.

Website visits

Website visits: the website bilingua.kalmarium.com counts page views with a cookie-free script that stores nothing on your device and sends only the page address, the referring website and the country (derived from your network address, which is not kept). It is switched off if your browser sends Do Not Track.

Earlier versions

This policy describes version 5.12. Until 5.12 reaches your phone, versions 5.11 and earlier differ in these ways:

  • looking up missing covers online was on by default;
  • the offline translation library bundled a Microsoft diagnostic component, which may send technical data about the device and the library's performance to Microsoft (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement), never the text you read;
  • the "Translate in Bilingua Reader" pop-up did not carry the AI-generated label;
  • the Google account address appeared in full in the optional debug log;
  • there was no "Report this answer" button.
